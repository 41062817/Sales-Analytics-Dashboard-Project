# Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes company sales data to identify key revenue drivers, regional performance, and product sales trends. A Power BI dashboard was developed to support business decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## Business Problem

Management lacked a centralized view of sales performance across regions and product categories, making it difficult to monitor performance and identify growth opportunities.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)
* Power BI (Dashboard Development)
* GitHub (Project Portfolio)

## Key Features

* Total Sales KPI indicator
* Sales by Region analysis
* Product performance comparison
* Monthly sales trend visualization
* Interactive dashboard filters

## Key Insights

* Gauteng generated the highest revenue contribution.
* Laptop products accounted for the largest share of total sales.
* Some regions showed low sales contribution, presenting growth opportunities.

## Business Recommendations

* Increase marketing efforts in high-performing regions.
* Expand promotional campaigns in underperforming regions.
* Implement cross-selling strategies for accessory products.
